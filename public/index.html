The Problem

Counselling is broken.
Here are the seven traps.

Most students pick their IIT on closing rank spreadsheets alone. Rankly layers 8 life-quality factors on top of real JoSAA data to eliminate costly mistakes.

01

Rank ≠ Happiness

A higher cutoff doesn't guarantee a better 4 years. Culture, location, mess quality, and annual fees shape your daily reality.

02

Cutoff Volatility

Cutoffs shift unpredictably year over year. Yesterday's safe choice can tighten. We track 5 years of final-round closing shifts (2021–2025) across OPEN, EWS, OBC, SC, and ST quotas.

03

Information Overload

23 IITs, 229 academic programs, and 12,947 closing cutoff data points. Thousands of pairs to parse manually while the JoSAA clock ticks down.

04

Zero Personalisation

Standard calculators treat every candidate the same. Rankly custom-weights your preferences—from tuition budget comfort to distance from home.

05

Opaque Black-Box Advice

Generic algorithms tell you where to apply without proof. Every fit score on Rankly has a transparent, 8-factor auditable breakdown.

06

Scattered Workflow

Juggling WhatsApp advice, 20 open browser tabs, and paper notes. Rankly gives you a centralized compare view, multi-factor scoring, and decision cockpit.

07

Choice Locking Panic

Submitting the final JoSAA order in a rush leads to irreversible mistakes. Rankly generates an ordered preference list with live Safe, Moderate, and Reach risk tiers.

Data & Methodology

Real cutoffs.
Transparent models.

Every recommendation is computed from final-round JoSAA closing ranks (2021–2025) across all 23 IITs, paired with normalized multi-factor preference scoring.

12,947 Cutoff Records (2021–2025)
23 IIT Campuses
229 Academic Programs
8 Decision Fit Factors
What comes directly from JoSAA
All opening and closing ranks (2021–2025) are extracted directly from official central JoSAA counselling datasets. We evaluate dual-pool seat matrices across Gender-Neutral and Female-Only supernumerary quotas for OPEN, EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, and ST categories.
What data is indicative
Tuition fee slabs, average placement CTCs, and campus infrastructure ratings are compiled from public institutional submissions and campus disclosures as indicative baselines. 2026 cutoff projections are mathematical estimates, not guarantees.
How Safe, Moderate, Reach & trends are computed
Safe indicates historical closing rank well above candidate rank (>90% likelihood). Moderate reflects cutoffs within striking range (~40–80%). Reach indicates aspirational options. Cutoff velocity uses Theil-Sen robust median slope analysis across 5 years to detect genuine demand shifts without noise distortion.
